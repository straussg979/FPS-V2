# COLD SECTOR: Afghanistan

This update moves the game to Afghanistan. The old Kestrel Compound is replaced by two new maps. Both are
built from a new set of more detailed buildings and vehicles, and both work in every mode: bot matches,
lobbies, public servers and ranked duels (site A). The bots need no setup: their navmesh and tactical map are
built from the new geometry on first start.

![Qalat Village from above](docs/afghanistan/qalat_overview.jpg)

## What's new at a glance

* **Two maps.** Qalat Village (`qalat`, the new default) and Sarai Bazaar (`bazaar`). Each has two bomb sites,
  attack and defend spawns, buy zones, 32–33 callouts, 6 defender cameras, bot attack routes and practice
  spots, and a 1v1 duel layout.
* **More detailed buildings.** Mud-brick houses now have plinths, timber ceiling beams that poke through the
  walls, lintels over doors and windows, shutters, iron window bars, door leaves, roof spouts with rain streaks,
  rounded parapets, brick domes and clutter on the roofs. There are also fort walls with buttresses, gates and
  eroded tops, round and square watchtowers, a two-storey hotel and a fuel station.
* **More detailed vehicles.** Each vehicle has real wheel arches, rims and wheel nuts, glass, mirrors, lights,
  bumpers, door seams and its own cargo. The set: Toyota Hilux pickups, technicals with a roll bar and a
  mounted machine gun, Corolla taxis, painted jingle trucks with their crown and chains of bells, a fuel tanker,
  motorbikes, a burnt-out BTR and a rusting T-55.
* **An in-game briefing.** The loading screen shows the map's name, region and description, what each side
  has to do, and what sites A and B are.
* **A Maps section on the Play page.** It has a card for each map, and the map menus show map names
  instead of file keys.
* **Public servers** rotate between the two maps: *Qalat #1* starts on the village, *Bazaar #1* on the town.
* **New soldiers** for both teams, and **no preparation wait** when you attack bots. See *Update 2* below.
* **49 new procedural materials**, all generated by the build like the existing ones: mud render, mud brick,
  fieldstone, weathered timber, painted doors, jingle-truck paints, carpets, tarps, foliage, rock and more. No
  downloads are needed.

## Update 2: new soldiers and no waiting on attack

### The soldiers

The old balaclava-and-box soldier is replaced by two factions dressed for the setting, each with a real face:
nose, brows, eyes, ears and short hair, in one of four skin tones per soldier. Some soldiers have a beard and
moustache. Each soldier keeps the same face for the whole match.

* **Vanguard** (team 1, starts on attack): arid multicam fatigues. They wear a high-cut coyote helmet with side
  rails, an NVG mount and a counterweight, a comms headset with a boom mic, and ballistic glasses. A shemagh is
  bunched round the neck. The plate carrier has a cummerbund, mag pouches (with the mags showing), an admin
  pouch, a hydration pack and a radio with its antenna. They also have gloves, knee pads and tan boots.
* **Bastion** (team 2, starts on defence): olive camo field jackets and a wool **pakol** cap. A grey scarf with a
  loose end hangs over the chest. The chest rig holds four AK mag pouches on straps that cross on the back. They
  have bare hands, a canteen pouch and brown boots.
* Both factions have shirts with collars, cuffs and shoulder pockets, trousers with cargo pockets, belts with
  buckles, laced boots with soles and toe caps, and fingers and thumbs that follow the hand bones.
* The first-person arms match your team: gloves and arid sleeves for Vanguard, bare hands and olive sleeves for
  Bastion.
* A fix: the old generator wound the body's surfaces inside out. The game drew the inside of the far side of
  each body tube, which made the soldiers look flat. The new model is closed and lit correctly.

Teams keep their faction when they swap sides at halftime. The model is still generated by the build
(`scripts/assets/soldier.ts`), with the same skeleton and hit boxes, so animations and hit registration are
unchanged.

### No preparation phase when you attack

When you're attacking and only bots are defending, the 20-second preparation phase is skipped. The round
goes straight from buy time to live. At that moment the defending bots are already in position: each bot's
site walls are reinforced and it stands at its hold spot, facing its angle. Only bots with a wire, sensor,
shield or jammer then go and place it.

* When a human is defending (you after halftime, or a friend in a lobby), the preparation phase stays, so
  they can reinforce and set up themselves.
* To turn this off, set `"skip_prep_vs_bot_defenders": false` in `data/match.json`.

Vehicles are `vehicle` pieces with a `kind`. The old `truck` and `utility` kinds still work.

| kind | Options |
|---|---|
| `hilux` | `mat` paint, `cargo` (`sacks`, `cans`, `tire`, `none`), `bullbar`, `tailgate_down`, `wreck` |
| `technical` | Same as `hilux`, plus a roll bar and a pedestal machine gun (`gun_heading`) |
| `sedan` / `taxi` | `mat` body, `mat2` roof (taxi: yellow with a white top), `taxi` roof sign, `roof_load`, `wreck` |
| `jingle` | `mat` cab colour, `load` (`sacks`, `tarp`, `empty`), `seed` for the panel pattern |
| `tanker` | `mat` cab, `tank_mat`, `wreck` |
| `motorbike` | `mat`, `lean` |
| `btr` | Burnt 8-wheeler on its rims: `pitch`, `roll`, `seed` |
| `t55` | Rusting tank: `turret` angle, `gun_pitch`, `thrown_track` |

## The building pieces

| Piece | What it makes | Main options |
|---|---|---|
| `mud_house` | A mud-brick house built on `building`, with all the trim described above | `min`/`max`, `height`, `openings` (`s`/`n`/`e`/`w` lists like `building`), `domes` `[{x, y, r}]`, `roof_items` (`tank`, `dish`, `wood`, `laundry`, `antenna`), `parapet_gaps`, `door_mat`, `shutter_mat`, `beams`, `plinth`, `spouts` |
| `qala_wall` | A thick fort wall | `p0`/`p1`, `height`, `thickness`, `openings` (`"gate": true` adds timber leaves), `merlons`, `buttress`, `damage` (eroded notches), `footing` |
| `watchtower` | A tapering tower (round or square) with loopholes and merlons | `base`, `size`, `height`, `round`, `taper` |
| `rock_wall` | The cliff of boulders that closes the map | `p0`/`p1` (outside on the right-hand side), `height`, `depth` |

| `shop` | A container shop or a mud stall, open on its local front, with an awning, a counter and goods | `style` (`container`, `mud`), `goods` (`fruit`, `rugs`, `tires`, `cans`, `cloth`, `sacks`), `awning`, `sign` |

## Working on the maps

* The maps are generated: edit `scripts/maps/qalat.py` or `scripts/maps/bazaar.py`, then run
  `python scripts/maps/qalat.py data/maps/qalat.json` (needs Python 3, nothing else).
* Check a map with `npx tsx scripts/maps/check.ts data/maps/qalat.json data/maps/bazaar.json --radar .`. It
  checks the paths from both spawns to every site, every bot route leg by leg, and that every room can be
  reached. It also writes the radar image.
* `npm start` rebuilds the map geometry, lighting and radar when a map file changes. New materials only cost
  build time the first time; their textures are cached in `build/cache`.

## Checks run on this update

* `npm run typecheck`: clean.
* `scripts/maps/check.ts` on both maps: every spawn reaches both sites, all 11 (Qalat) and 12 (Bazaar) bot routes
  are walkable, all practice positions are on the navmesh, and no building has a sealed room.
* A full `npm run build`, then the server: both public servers start with 10 bots each. Watched bot matches on
  both maps had bots buying, rotating, calling out the new areas, planting at A on Qalat and winning a round at
  the hotel on Sarai Bazaar.
